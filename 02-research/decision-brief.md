# Decision Brief — Streakly Comeback
**To:** Marcus, Head of Product | **From:** Engagement Squad | **For:** Thursday kickoff

## Situation
Day-7 retention fell from 48% to 39% after the v2 redesign, and three independent research sources — user interviews, NPS free-text, and the competitive scan — converge on the same root cause: the streak mechanic has no state between "intact" and "zero." We need to pick a fix concept to bring into Thursday's kickoff.

## Key Findings
- **The all-or-nothing streak reset is the single highest-leverage issue.** It's the top NPS theme (6/10 comments) and the explicit reason a churned interview subject (Tom) gave for leaving.
- **Loss-aversion anxiety starts on day 4, before any habit has formed.** New user Amara is already doing the same loss math that broke Tom — the product asks users to feel invested in something fragile long before it gives them a reason the risk is worth it.
- **Positive reinforcement doesn't show up until day ~30** (Priya's celebration moment), leaving a "fragile middle zone" (day 4–30) with pride to lose but nothing new to gain.
- **No competitor owns a truly free, non-punitive comeback experience.** Duolingo paywalls its freeze/repair; Babbel avoids the problem by de-emphasizing streaks entirely; Elevate, Streaks, and Habitica offer no real recovery — this is open white space.
- **Notifications and re-engagement compound the problem**, not cause it: poorly targeted pings get disabled, removing the one channel that could pull lapsing users back — but this is secondary to the core mechanic.

## Options Considered
1. **Duolingo-style streak freeze/repair (paywalled or currency-gated).** Proven mechanic, but users are explicitly asking for a coach, not a monetized escape hatch — and it doesn't address the day 4–30 anxiety gap.
2. **Babbel-style de-emphasis of streaks.** Removes the punishment but also removes the ritual that made Priya's experience stick — throws away the mechanic's proven upside.
3. **Free-by-default comeback flow + early milestones, paired with a state-aware home screen.** Directly targets the two things every source names: no recovery path, and no acknowledgment of where a user actually is. Unclaimed by any competitor.

## Recommended Action
Build a free, non-punitive streak-recovery flow plus early (pre-day-7) milestone moments and a state-aware home screen, rather than a paywalled freeze or de-emphasizing streaks altogether.

## Why Now
This is the one fix concept that is simultaneously the top-cited churn driver (NPS + interviews), unaddressed by any competitor, and directly answers the interview insight that week 1 needs something to feel proud of before the fragile day 4–30 window — making it the highest-confidence, highest-differentiation option to bring into Thursday's kickoff.
