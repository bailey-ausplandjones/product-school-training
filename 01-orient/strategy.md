# Strategy — Streakly Comeback

*Where this fits in the bigger picture. Starting point, not finished — TBD items to confirm Thursday.*

## The Drop

Day-7 retention fell 9 points, from 48% to 39%, after the v2 streak redesign. The drop is sharpest among users who break their streak in week 1; missing two days in a row roughly doubles churn.

## Key Tension

Streak as motivator vs. streak-break as punishment. The streak mechanic drives the habit loop, but today a broken streak resets to zero with no acknowledgment, and the "you lost your streak" push has a harsh tone and offers nothing when tapped.

## Hypothesis

Users go passive after breaking a streak because the app treats the break as a cold reset rather than offering a graceful way back in. Recovering the 9-point drop likely means giving users a comeback path specific to their own progress, not a generic "keep going" message.

Open, unresolved: how much of the drop is driven by the reset experience itself vs. notification timing/tone.
